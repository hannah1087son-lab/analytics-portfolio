# Hotel Review Text Analytics

## Business Problem

Hotel reviews contain valuable customer feedback, but the volume and
unstructured format make recurring service issues difficult to identify
manually.

## Approach

Used topic-modeling techniques, including LDA and BERTopic, to organize
guest feedback into interpretable themes.

## Key Themes

-   Cleanliness, staff, and walkability
-   Location, views, and amenities
-   Front desk, check-in, and service recovery
-   Room access, bathrooms, and comfort
-   Lobby, bar, and atmosphere

## Business Takeaway

The analysis translated unstructured reviews into actionable
customer-experience themes, highlighting the importance of service
execution and feedback loops alongside strong amenities and location.

## Tools

Python \| NLP \| LDA \| BERTopic \| Text Analytics
